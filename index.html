01 / The combat

Make every
hit count.

Your fighter moves with physics. So does the opponent. Read their wind-up, find an opening and stay on your feet when the next hit lands.

I

Swing with intent

Charge your strike, choose one of four directions and change your wind-up to fool a guard. Swords, axes and hammers each bring a different kind of trouble.

II

Get hands-on

Kick a rival off balance, grab them and drag left or right to choose your throw. A well-timed shield block can turn a wild swing into your opening.

III

Take a tumble

Stagger, fall and fight your way back up. Shields wear down and break; heavy cuts can sever limbs. A duel can change with a single clash.

02 / The journey

Small beginnings.
Royal ambitions.

Start at the Village Fair. Earn your place across ten tournaments and fight for the title of King's Champion. Each tournament is an eight-fighter bracket: three duels stand between you and the trophy.

  1. 01–03

    Earn your name

    Village Fair · Harvest Festival · Market Town Melee

  2. 04–07

    Face the lords

    Baron's Games · Lord's Arena · Duke's Tourney · Border Wars Cup

  3. 08–10

    Claim the crown

    Crown Trials · Grand Coliseum · King's Tournament

03 / Between the bouts

Build your kind of fighter.

Return to your training ground, try your gear on the dummy and prepare for the next rival. Train Strength, Agility, Endurance and Reflex to shape the way you fight.

Gold
Buy weapons, shields and helmets at the blacksmith.
Fame
Unlock the next tournament by proving yourself in the arena.
Skill points
Develop your stats and find a fighting style that feels like yours.
