## Google Play mağaza metinleri

Metinler oyunun diliyle uyumlu olarak İngilizcedir. Aşağıdakiler mağazaya
aktarılacak taslaklardır.

### Uygulama adı (en fazla 30 karakter)

Ragdoll Gladiator

### Kısa açıklama (en fazla 80 karakter)

Swing, kick and throw your way to glory in physics-based medieval duels.

### Tam açıklama (en fazla 4000 karakter)

A little wobbly. A lot of fight.

Enter Ragdoll Gladiator, a medieval fighting game where every swing, block and tumble is driven by physics. Face AI-controlled rivals in single-player 1v1 duels and work your way from the Village Fair to the King's Tournament.

MAKE EVERY HIT COUNT

Hold to charge a strike, then release it when you see an opening. Swing from four directions and change your wind-up to mislead a guard. Raise your shield, kick an opponent off balance, or grab a rival and choose the direction of your throw.

FIGHT WITH PHYSICS

Heavy hits make fighters stagger. Follow-up strikes can send them tumbling, but falling is not always the end: get back on your feet and return to the fight. Shields wear down and break. Sharp weapons can sever limbs, changing a duel in an instant.

BUILD YOUR FIGHTER

Return to your training ground between bouts. Spend gold at the blacksmith on swords, axes, hammers, shields and helmets. Longer weapons reach farther; heavier equipment changes how you move and fight. Train Strength, Agility, Endurance and Reflex with skill points, then test your gear on the training dummy.

FROM VILLAGE BRAWLER TO KING'S CHAMPION

Earn fame to open new tournaments and win each title to advance. Ten tournaments lead from village fairs through the lords' arenas to the King's Tournament. Each eight-fighter bracket gives you three duels to win the trophy. Meet named rivals with different fighting styles and build your collection of titles.

FEATURES

• Physics-based medieval 1v1 combat against AI rivals
• Charged strikes, four slash directions and feints
• Shield blocks, kicks, grabs and directional throws
• Physical reactions, falls and recovery
• Swords, axes, hammers, shields and helmets
• Four trainable stats that shape your fighting style
• Ten tournaments across five arena settings
• A training ground, blacksmith, armory and trophy collection
• Local progress saving
• Touch controls designed for landscape play on Android

Contains stylized violence, blood and dismemberment.

## Reklam ve satın alım

Android sürümlerinde maç arası reklamlar ve isteğe bağlı ödüllü reklamlar
bulunabilir. `remove_ads_permanent` tek seferlik reklam kaldırma ürünüdür;
başka oyundaki satın alım gold bonusu burada vaat edilmez. Fiyat, ürünün
kapsamı ve varsa ödüllü reklamın faydası ilgili ekranda gösterilir.
