Ragdoll Gladiator Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ragdoll Gladiator ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our Android game.

1. Information We Collect

Ragdoll Gladiator does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or payment card information to play the game.

Depending on the features you use, the following information may be processed:

  • Local gameplay data: preferences, sound settings, progress, unlocked content, virtual currency, purchased entitlements, and other in-game state stored on your device.
  • Advertising data: approximate location inferred from an IP address, app interactions such as app launches, taps, ad interactions and video views, diagnostic information, Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers.
  • Analytics data: a randomized installation identifier, approximate country or region inferred from an IP address, device and operating-system information, app sessions, screen views, gameplay events such as duel results and tournament progression, crash logs, errors, and related diagnostic information.
  • Purchase history: product identifier, purchase token, purchase status, purchase time, and order or transaction identifier where available. Google Play processes your payment method and billing information; we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Other user-generated content: a star rating and any review text you choose to submit through the Google Play in-app review flow.

2. How We Use Information

We and our service providers use information to:

  • Operate the game and remember your settings and progress.
  • Process, verify, restore, and prevent fraud involving purchases.
  • Display, personalize where permitted, measure, and improve ads.
  • Analyze performance, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse.
  • Allow you to submit and manage a Google Play review.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Android releases of Ragdoll Gladiator may use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and share the advertising data described above for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Ads may be personalized based on your consent, region, device settings, and Google's policies. If personalized advertising is unavailable or not permitted, non-personalized or limited ads may still be shown.

Where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app will request consent before using personal data or device storage for personalized advertising. You can review or change available privacy choices through the consent message shown in the app and through your Android or Google ad settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: Google Business Data Responsibility.

4. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

Android releases may use GameAnalytics to understand how the game is used and to identify technical problems. GameAnalytics processes a randomized installation identifier, device information, approximate country or region, app interactions, other gameplay actions, crash logs, errors, and diagnostics on our behalf. We use this information for usage analytics, session and progression analysis, debugging, security, and improving the game. We do not use GameAnalytics to collect your name or email address.

Review the GameAnalytics Privacy Notice and Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and billing information under its own privacy policy and terms. The app receives limited purchase information needed to complete and verify the transaction, grant or restore the purchased item, keep purchase records, provide support, and detect fraud. Purchase entitlements may also be stored locally on your device.

6. In-App Reviews (Google Play)

If you choose to use the in-app review feature, Google Play collects the rating and review text you submit. Reviews may be displayed publicly on Google Play. During closed testing, review data may instead be shared privately with us. You can edit or delete a review through your Google Play Store or Google Account.

7. Other Google Play Services

Ragdoll Gladiator is distributed through Google Play. Google Play and related Google services may process install, device, purchase, review, security, and diagnostic information under Google's own policies. Review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Approximate location, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers may be collected and shared with Google and its advertising partners through AdMob for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Review content is processed by Google Play and may be displayed publicly or, during closed testing, shared privately with us.

Analytics data is processed by GameAnalytics as a service provider acting on our behalf. We use it for analytics, debugging, security, and improving Ragdoll Gladiator, and not for selling your personal information.

Google Play Billing processes purchase transactions initiated by you and provides the app with limited purchase records needed to deliver, verify, and restore purchases. We may also disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect users, the app, or our legal rights.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Gameplay data, preferences, and purchase entitlements may be stored locally on your device. Local data is generally removed when you uninstall the app, subject to Android backup and restore settings. Google retains information processed through AdMob and Google Play according to its own retention policies and legal obligations. GameAnalytics retains and deletes data according to its linked Data Retention and Deletion Policy and applicable legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information, or withdraw consent. You may:

  • Change advertising and privacy settings on your Android device or Google Account.
  • Reset or delete your Android advertising ID through device settings.
  • Change consent choices through the privacy options provided in the app, where available.
  • Edit or delete Google Play reviews through Google Play or your Google Account.
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored data, subject to Android backup settings.
  • Contact us using the details below regarding applicable privacy rights.

11. Data Deletion Requests

Ragdoll Gladiator does not create user accounts. To request deletion of personal data that we control, email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject "Ragdoll Gladiator Data Deletion Request." Do not include payment card details or other sensitive financial information in your request. We will respond within a reasonable period and delete applicable data unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes. Where applicable, we will also forward a verified request to service providers that process data on our behalf, including GameAnalytics.

Data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup settings. Reviews and other data controlled by Google can be managed through your Google Play Store, Google Account, Android privacy settings, or by contacting Google under its privacy policy.

12. Children's Privacy

Ragdoll Gladiator is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that does not comply with applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete it. If you believe this has occurred, please contact us.

13. Security and International Processing

We use reasonable measures to protect information associated with the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their policies and applicable legal safeguards.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

15. This Website

These static pages do not include advertising SDKs, analytics scripts, account registration, or tracking cookies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, to serve and secure the site under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Opening Google Play or another external link is subject to that service's own privacy policy. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include so that we can respond.
